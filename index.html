PandoraDrop

One-time, self-destructing message links

PandoraDrop encrypts your message in your browser (AES-256-GCM via the Web Crypto API) before it ever leaves your device. The decryption key lives only in the share link's URL fragment, never sent to any server - the backend stores ciphertext it cannot read, and deletes it the moment it's read once.

What this is - and isn't

  • A real, live one-time encrypted link tool - not a full messaging platform.
  • A custom Web Crypto implementation, not the independently-audited Signal protocol.
  • No accounts, no persistent chat history, no AI features, no decentralized network.
